CDF Calculator

Calculate the probability of getting k or fewer successes.

Integer from 0 to n
P(X ≤ k)
—
P(X ≤ k) = Σ P(X = i), i = 0 to k
P(X > k)
—
P(X > k) = 1 - P(X ≤ k)

P(X ≤ k) means the probability of getting k or fewer successes.

PMF Calculator

For X ~ Binomial(n, p), calculate the probability of exactly k successes.

Integer from 0 to n
Probability
—
P(X=k) = C(n,k) p^k (1-p)^(n-k)

This is the probability of getting exactly k successes in n independent trials.

PMF Calculator

For X ~ Poisson(λ), calculate the probability of exactly k events.

Non-negative integer
Probability
—
P(X=k) = e^(-λ) λ^k / k!

This is the probability of observing exactly k events when the average number of events is λ.

PMF Calculator

For X ~ Geometric(p), where X is the trial number of the first success.

Positive integer, k ≥ 1
Probability
—
P(X=k) = p(1-p)^(k-1)

This is the probability that the first success occurs on trial k.

CDF Calculator

Calculate the probability that the first success occurs by trial k.

Positive integer, k ≥ 1
P(X ≤ k)
—
P(X ≤ k) = 1 - (1-p)^k
P(X > k)
—
P(X > k) = (1-p)^k

P(X ≤ k) means the probability that the first success occurs on or before trial k.
